Why HealthTrack

Everything scattered, nothing connected

Most people have five apps and zero answers. HealthTrack puts the pieces together and keeps them that way.

One timeline for everything

Wearables, lab PDFs, medications and symptoms land in a single chronological view — so a bad week is something you can actually see instead of something you try to remember.

Trends you can actually read

No raw exports or dashboards full of noise. HealthTrack compares each metric against your own baseline and flags what changed — and roughly how much it changed.

Private by default

Your records are encrypted at rest and in transit, and never sold. Share a read-only summary with a clinician when you choose to — and revoke it whenever you want.

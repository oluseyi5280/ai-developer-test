FAQ

Questions before you connect a source

Still unsure about something? Send us a note and a human on the product team will answer — usually the same day.

How long does setup actually take?

Connecting a helpdesk or Slack channel takes a couple of minutes. Historical imports run in the background, so you can start triaging new feedback immediately and watch the archive fill in behind it.

Where does Beacon pull feedback from?

Zendesk, Intercom, HubSpot, Slack, email forwarding, CSV import, app store reviews via the API, and any custom source you can reach with our REST API or webhooks.

Will our customer data be used to train models?

No. Your data is used to generate clusters and summaries for your workspace only, and it is never pooled into training data for other customers. Enterprise plans can disable AI features entirely.

Can we keep our roadmap private?

Yes. The public roadmap is a separate, opt-in view. You choose exactly which items appear there, and everything else stays internal by default.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your workspace drops to the Starter plan automatically — nothing is deleted, and you keep read access to everything you imported. Upgrade whenever you're ready.
